Sheets from Views

Makes a sheet of any view in the drawing: the whole cave, each profile, each cross section. Tick the ones you want and pick the paper; each becomes its own sheet, scaled to fit its view, with only its own layers showing. layoutviews

When you reach for it

You want a sheet of one profile, or of a cross section, without building it by hand. Sheet Setup makes the plan and one elevation; this lists everything the drawing holds.

What you get

  • The cave's plan has a north arrow; profiles and cross sections do not.
  • Each sheet has the title block, scale bar and border from Sheet Setup.
  • A sheet you changed by hand is left alone the next time.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It makes layouts only; it never changes the cave drawing itself.
