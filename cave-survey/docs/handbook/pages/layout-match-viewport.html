Match Viewport

Copies one viewport's scale and rotation to others: click the viewport to copy from, then click each one to change; Esc finishes. Where each shows (the view's centre) is not copied. matchviewport

When you reach for it

A plan and its elevation, or several detail views that must agree.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
