Draw a ledge or pit so the hachures face the right way

Hachures point down the drop. Backwards, the map says the water runs uphill.

Steps

  1. Arm the shaped line: Floor Ledge, Ceiling Ledge, Pit, Flowstone, Rimstone Dam or Slope.
  2. Drag along the edge — the lip, not the foot.
  3. Release. The ornament follows your cursor until you click the low side: the side hachures hang down, scallops bow toward, slope fans splay onto.
  4. Continuing a shaped line of the same style grows it, spine and ornament together, with the side inherited — you are not asked again.

Getting it wrong, and fixing it

  • Flip Shaped Side (shf) mirrors the ornament on a line already drawn.
  • Check Map flags a floor ledge whose hachures the nearest floor elevations say are on the high side. It is a suggestion, not a verdict — it reads the floor levels near the line.
  • Edit the spine with QCAD's own tools and the ornament regenerates to follow.

In the elevation

The same buttons work there: a stroke inside a profile band's box lands on the PROFILE- twin layers automatically.
