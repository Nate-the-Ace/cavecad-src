Correct a bad reading without forking a duplicate trip

A transposed azimuth, a tape read as 12.4 that was 42.4. Fixing it has one right door and several wrong ones.

Steps

  1. Survey Notebook, then Load from drawing and pick the trip.
  2. Fix the number in the ladder.
  3. Draw. Because the page was loaded from the drawing, it revises that trip by identity.

Loading from the drawing is what matters. Retyping the trip by hand and drawing it again matches on date and team, so a trip whose date you also corrected arrives as a second trip and the cave is drawn twice.

Correcting the trip itself

Name, date, team or instruments: ... > Edit this trip.... It edits by identity, moves nothing, needs no redraw, and is one undo step.

Declination is different

Changing declination rotates the plan, so it is not among those fields. It lives in Declination, and the drawing rotates around the fix.

Try it on Truitt

Correct a trip's date from Edit this trip..., then count the trips. The count must be unchanged.
