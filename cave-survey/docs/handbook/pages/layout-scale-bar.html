Add Scale Bar

Puts a scale bar on the layout. Click where its baseline starts. It measures the viewport under it and follows that viewport's scale whenever you change it, zoom inside it, or undo. scalebar

When you reach for it

When you drew a viewport yourself, or deleted the sheet's bar. A viewport has one bar; to move it, delete it and place another.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only: in the cave itself it tells you to click a layout tab first.

It is the person's, not the generator's: Sheet Setup never rewrites or deletes a piece placed here.
