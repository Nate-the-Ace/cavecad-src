Reset Drawing

Empty a cave drawing completely, for a fresh start. rd


Reset stays disabled until the cave's name is typed. Counts shown are invented.

When you reach for it

When the drawing is past saving and you want to draw it again over the same scans. Not for starting a new cave — that is New Cave Map.

What goes, and what stays

Everything in the drawing goes: the survey, the traced linework, the symbols, the notes, the sections, the placed sketch scans, the aerial basemap and the georeference. Every one of those is put there by a tool you are here to learn, so leaving any behind would skip that lesson.

The cave's folder is not touched — every scan, photograph, capture and PDF stays on disk, ready to be placed again. That is what makes emptying the drawing survivable. Three things outside the drawing go with it: the Complete marks on this cave's scans, the last-declared location, and the shelf card's thumbnail. The shelf entry itself stays.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Reset Drawing.
  2. A backup is written before you are asked anything.
  3. Type the cave's name to confirm.
  4. A progress window names each phase. There is no cancel: stopping half way leaves a drawing that is neither the map it was nor the blank it was going to be.
  5. Nothing is saved. Close without saving and the reset itself is undone.

When it refuses

  • A backup that will not write stops the tool before anything is deleted.
