Fill the floor with a pattern

Gravel, sand, breakdown, water, flowstone and eight others, as a boundary and the fill derived from it.

Steps

  1. Open the Areas section of the Draw panel.
  2. Pick a pattern. Each tile is a picture of what that pattern actually draws.
  3. Press and drag to enclose the area. Release: boundary and fill arrive together, in one undo step.
  4. Set Scale and Density before the drag to size and thin what gets placed.

Changing it afterwards

Drag a grip, move the boundary, warp it — the fill regenerates to match. Delete the boundary and the fill goes with it. The scatter's seed is kept for the life of the boundary, so its look survives an edit, an undo, a reload, or a redraw on someone else's machine.

When it refuses

  • Density greys out for a filled pattern — a hatch has nothing scattered in it to thin.
  • A fill that has not followed its boundary means the live listener is not running: Sync Areas rebuilds by hand.
