Check Sheet

Checks the layout you are looking at before it goes out, and says what it finds, most serious first: images that would print (an aerial photograph shows where the cave is), viewports that show nothing, a legend sitting in the map, no north arrow, no scale bar, no title block, text too small to read, and notes for viewports that are unlocked or at a non-standard scale. It changes nothing. checksheet

When you reach for it

Before Plot Layout, and after you have moved things about by hand.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
