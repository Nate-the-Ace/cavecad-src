Where a cave's files live

The project is the folder, not the drawing. Everything else is stored relative to it.

Inside a cave folder

  • <Cave>.dxf — the drawing: survey, linework, symbols, sections, the elevation.
  • scans/ — scanned field book pages, usually one folder per trip. What Sketch Scans and the Notebook both browse.
  • PDF/ — plotted maps.
  • images/ — photographs.
  • backup/ — what the suite writes before anything destructive.

Beside your caves

  • ~/Documents/Cave/symbols/CaveCustomSymbols.dxf — your own symbols, where no CaveCAD update can reach them.
  • ~/Documents/Cave/templates/ — the NSS templates.
  • ~/Documents/Cave/depot/ — the zips Package Cave writes.
  • ~/Documents/Cave/teaching/master/ — the teaching cave's pristine master.

Why it matters

A scan is remembered as a file reference, so moving or renaming pages outside CaveCAD blanks them in the drawing — fixable, but a detour. Keep pages inside the cave's own scans/ folder and drag new ones onto the Sketch Scans preview, which copies them into place for you.
