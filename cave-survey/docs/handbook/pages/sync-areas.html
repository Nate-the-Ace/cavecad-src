Sync Areas

Rebuild every area's fill by hand. sya

When you reach for it

The manual fallback: a drawing edited elsewhere, one where the live listener never installed, or a boundary moved by a script. Normally the fill follows its boundary on its own and you never need this.

What else it does

It adopts every old BREAKDOWN-BOUNDARY polyline Scatter Breakdown drew that has no AreaId yet: tags it, keeps its existing pile's seed so the look does not change, and rebuilds it as a real area. Run once per boundary; harmless to run again.

It is also the one place a custom area pattern's missing block is fetched into the drawing on your behalf — the live listener deliberately never does that on its own.
