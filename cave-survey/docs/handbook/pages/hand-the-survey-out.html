Write the survey back out

The shots, in somebody else's format.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Export Cave Survey.
  2. Name the file with the suffix you want — the format follows the name.

It exports what the map actually shows, including everything typed into the Notebook since the import, and leaves the cave's fixed station control out unless you ask for it.

All of them at once

Package Cave Project writes every interchange format together with the drawing, the PDFs and a manifest, as one zip — and sanitizes by default.
