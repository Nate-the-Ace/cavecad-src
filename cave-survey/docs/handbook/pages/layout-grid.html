Add Grid

Puts tick marks and labels just outside the selected rectangular viewport (not turned, not shaped). By default the labels are distances from the cave's south-west corner (0, 100, 200 ...), because a map must not carry where the cave is. True map coordinates are offered too, behind a confirmation, for the rare plot that wants them. Adding it again replaces the earlier grid. addgrid

When you reach for it

A plan that needs something to measure positions by.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
