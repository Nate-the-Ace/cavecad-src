Loop closure and adjustment

Walk a loop in a cave and survey your way back to where you started, and the survey will not land you exactly there. The gap is the loop's misclosure.

What the percentage means

"Closes at 2.4%" says the miss is 2.4% of the distance walked round the loop. It is not a grade you passed or failed. A 2.4% miss on a 40 ft loop is one bad reading; the same percentage on 1400 ft of survey is a different conversation.

What adjustment does

It shares the miss out among the stations in the loop, moving each a little so the loop closes. Every station in that loop is then drawn somewhere slightly different from where the raw shots put it — which stations, how far, and which way is the whole story, and it is invisible on the map.

Loop Errors draws it: one arrow per moved station, coloured by how far in feet, exaggerated by a factor printed beside them.

No loops

Most caves start with none, and that is not a fault. But nothing in a loopless survey is checking anything else: a transposed azimuth simply bends the cave and nothing objects.
