Hand a cave to someone else

One zip holding the drawing, the plotted maps, the survey in every format, and a manifest anyone can read without CaveCAD.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Package Cave Project.
  2. Choose sanitized or full.
  3. It writes into ~/Documents/Cave/depot, never plots a PDF, and never touches the original drawing.

Which one

  • Sanitized — the default, and the right answer for anyone outside the survey. The copied drawing loses its geographic anchor, and no aerial photograph travels.
  • Full — keeps both, and says so in its file name. Your own storage, or the next cartographer.

Never hand over the working drawing itself as a shortcut. It carries the anchor and, if you fetched imagery, a picture of the entrance.
