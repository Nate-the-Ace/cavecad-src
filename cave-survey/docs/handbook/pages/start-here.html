Start here

This handbook lives inside CaveCAD. Every page ships with the program, so it works in a cave hut with no signal exactly as it works on a desk.

How to read it

Press Contents at the foot of this panel for every page, grouped the way the Cave Survey menu is grouped. The back arrow at the top brings you back to what you were reading. Type a word in the search box and press Return.

Every panel in the suite carries a small ? in its corner. Pressing it opens this handbook at that panel's page, so you never have to go looking.

What is in here

  • Tool pages — one per entry in the Cave Survey menu. What it is, when you reach for it, what its refusals mean.
  • How do I... — recipes that cross several tools. Getting a scan to line up is here, not on any one tool's page.
  • What the words mean — station, shot, LRUD, datum, closure.
  • Lessons — six, in order, drawing a whole cave map start to finish. Begin at Lesson 1.

Try it on Truitt

The examples in this handbook are all on Truitt Cave, a real cave you can safely ruin. Cave Survey > Teaching Cave > Set up makes your copy; Reset puts it back afterwards. Do that now, then start Lesson 1.
