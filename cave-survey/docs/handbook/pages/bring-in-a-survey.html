Bring in a survey from other software

Compass .dat, Walls .srv, Survex .svx, Therion .th, or CSV. The format is detected for you.

A cave nobody has here yet

  1. Cave Survey > Caves, then Import Cave....
  2. It builds the whole project: the folder, scans/, PDF/, images/ and backup/, and a drawing on the NSS template with the survey already drawn.
  3. Import Folder... takes a cave folder, or a folder full of them, in one go.

Shots into a drawing you already have

  1. Open the drawing.
  2. Cave Survey > Import Cave Survey and pick the file.
  3. Check the trips that arrive in the Notebook — dates, teams, declination. An imported file often carries none of the three.

From a phone

TopoDroid and PocketTopo export Therion, so a phone survey comes in through the same door.
