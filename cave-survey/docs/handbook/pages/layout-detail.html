Add Detail

Adds a magnified circular view of part of the map. Pick the magnification (2x to 8x), click the centre of the area on a map viewport, click a point on its edge, then click where the detail goes. The map gets a lettered circle and a leader to the detail; the detail is a circular viewport at the map's own rotation, hiding the same layers, labelled "DETAIL A (x3)". Further details take B, C and so on. detail

When you reach for it

A junction or a room too busy to read at the sheet's scale.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
