Export Cave Survey

Write the drawing's survey back out as Compass, Walls, Survex, Therion or CSV. ecs

When you reach for it

When someone else needs the shots — in their own software, or for a state survey database.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Export Cave Survey.
  2. The format follows the name you save under, so give it the right suffix.

It exports what the map actually shows, including everything typed into the Notebook since the import, and leaves the cave's fixed station control out unless you ask for it.
