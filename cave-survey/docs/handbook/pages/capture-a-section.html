Capture a cross section, three routes

Cut it from the survey, trace it from your field book, or reopen one you traced before.

Cut it from the survey

  1. Cave Survey > Cross Section, take the default.
  2. Click a point on the passage, then click where the section should sit.
  3. Read the caption: it states the scale and how far the cut is from the nearer station that fed it. Beside a station it is nearly a measurement; midway between distant stations it is an interpolation.

Trace a scanned section

  1. Choose Trace a scanned section and pick the station.
  2. A bay opens clear of the plan: a locked frame holding the scan and a dashed ghost of that station's own computed LRUD outline.
  3. Scale the scan onto the ghost, then trace with the ordinary drawing tools.
  4. Capture sweeps what you traced inside the frame into its own block — never the scan, the ghost or the frame — leaders it home to the station, and proposes a placement clear of the walls. Take it with Enter, or click somewhere else.
  5. Cancel removes the frame, scan and ghost and leaves your linework where it is.

Reopen one you traced

Reopen a section I already traced puts a placed section's linework back loose in a fresh bay at the scale it was traced at, and removes the placed block so the section exists in only one place at a time. Capture puts it back.

When it refuses

  • A station with no cuttable LRUD still opens the bay, with no ghost, and says so.
  • Capture with nothing traced is refused with an explanation rather than making an empty block.
