Lay sketches on the passage in 3D

Your own scanned pages, draped onto the surveyed passage, so a sketch can be read against the shape it describes.

Steps

  1. Place and fit the scans first — a drape follows whatever the drawing already points at, including a trimmed derivative.
  2. Cave Survey > 3D View.
  3. Orbit. Plan sketches lie on the passage; profile sketches stand along it.

What it is doing

A plan sketch is a grid whose elevation is sampled from nearby stations, so it follows the passage under it rather than the cave's average depth. A profile sketch is drawn against an unrolled axis, so it is cut into strips — one per leg — and walked back onto the centreline: a single flat quad would cut every corner and lay the sketch through rock the passage goes around.

Only the pencil survives. Anything lighter than ink is discarded, so the paper does not become a wall in front of the cave.

When it refuses

  • Nothing from the drape is ever written back to the drawing. Inventing a surface to look at is not inventing survey data.
