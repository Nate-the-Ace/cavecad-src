Put a sheet together at a scale that fits

The map becomes paper: a border, a scale bar, a north arrow, a title block, on a tab of your own drawing.

Steps

  1. Run Check Map first and work its list down. A sheet built over a fault prints the fault.
  2. Cave Survey > Sheet Setup.
  3. Pick the paper. It proposes the most detailed standard plot scale the plan fits at — take it unless you have a reason.
  4. Tick the furniture. The preview redraws as you tick: the paper, the cave's footprint, the furniture, and where the elevation bands land.
  5. Read the warnings. It says plainly when something is off the paper and which thing.
  6. Build Sheet. The sheets appear as tabs next to Model. Look at each one, then Export PDF for the whole set in one file.
  7. Want to nudge something? Move it on the sheet. That sheet is then yours (starred on its tab) and later builds leave it alone; Revert to automatic on its tab takes it back.

Metric

The ratios at the bottom of the list — 1:100 to 1:2000 — are unit-free, and a metric sheet gets a bar counted in metres captioned with its ratio.

What never reaches a plot

No raster at all: not your scanned sketches, not the aerial photograph.
