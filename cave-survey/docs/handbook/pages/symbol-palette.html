When you reach for it

For everything that is a mark rather than a line: entrances, pits, domes, formations, flow arrows.

Biology, Archaeology, Hazards and Passage ends

Four categories for what a cave map has to warn about. Biology: bat, root, tree trunk, vegetable debris, seed germination. Archaeology: excavation, artefact, fossil, bones, human bones, masonry, altar, ex-voto. Hazards: danger, dig, air draught. Passage ends: continuation, low end, narrow end, and breakdown, clay and flowstone chokes.

Two of these ask for care. Mark a bat roost for your own group's use and leave it off any map that leaves the group, because a published roost is a disturbed one. Human bones are a burial: mark them, leave them, and report them to the landowner and the authorities instead of publicising them.

A continuation is a lead. It places a question mark on NOTES-DIG; give the reason it stopped in a callout beside it.

More formations, geology and equipment

Formations gains helictite, soda straw, pendant, cave pearls, crystal, aragonite, gypsum, gypsum flower, volcano and clay tree. Geology gains scallops, flutes, karren and anastomosis. The Rigging category gains bridge, walkway, handrail, steps, fixed ladder, gate, camp and name plate. A fixed ladder has straight rails; a hanging cable ladder keeps its wavy ones.

The Therion sketch importer places these too: every Therion point type that means something on a cave map now lands as a symbol, an area fill or a callout. The full table is docs/symbol-coverage-audit.md in the tools repository.

Rigging

The Rigging category is for how you get down the cave rather than what the cave is: Bolt, Y-hang, Rebelay, Deviation, Natural anchor, Rope, Cable ladder and Traverse line. Each one places at the point you click — the bolt itself for an anchor, the hang point for a Y, the rope head for a drop, the left-hand anchor for a traverse.

They all land on one layer, ANCHORS-BOLTS, so a map printed for someone who is not rigging the cave turns the whole rigging topo off in one click.

A pitch length belongs in a note beside the rope, not in the symbol: re-rig the pitch and the note changes while the symbol stays true.

New Symbol... files under Rigging like any other category — a maillon, a knot, a hanger your club draws differently. Pick Rigging in the category box and ANCHORS-BOLTS as the layer, and it lands beside the shipped rigging symbols and turns off with them.

Two of them wave on purpose. Rope and Cable ladder carry the wave that says this hangs; straight rails would mean a fixed ladder bolted to the rock, which is a different piece of equipment. Nothing crosses a rope with a stroke — a line with ticks across it reads as a break.

Steps

  1. Pick a symbol and click in the drawing to place it.
  2. Or press and drag instead: the symbol turns to face the cursor and takes its size from how far you dragged — the distance from the press point is its radius. Aimed and sized in one gesture.
  3. The next plain click repeats what the last drag asked for, so a row of six identical flow arrows is one drag and five clicks.
  4. Where you click decides the view, exactly as it does for Feature Trace. The readout above the tiles names the layer under the cursor before you press.
  5. Return with one symbol left showing arms it. The Recent strip holds the last five.

There are no size or angle boxes to type into: the drag sets both.

Your own symbols

New Symbol... opens a drawing to draw one in, centred on its origin with a 10 ft working square — the biggest a symbol is meant to be. Give it a name, a category and a home layer, and it is written into your own library at ~/Documents/Cave/symbols/CaveCustomSymbols.dxf, beside your caves, where it syncs and gets backed up with them and no CaveCAD update can reach it.

When it refuses

  • The symbols the suite ships cannot be edited or deleted — the next update would take the change back. Use Duplicate as New Symbol... and edit the copy.
  • A drawing that does not have a symbol — an old cave, or one never started from the template — gets the block fetched from the template as the symbol is placed.
