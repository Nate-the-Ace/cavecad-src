Lesson 6 — Sharing it

The last lesson is the one with consequences outside the room.

Do this

  1. Cave Survey > Export Cave Survey. Write the survey out as Compass, then again as Therion. Open one in a text editor and read it.
  2. Cave Survey > Package Cave Project, sanitized. Find the zip in ~/Documents/Cave/depot.
  3. Open the manifest inside it. Everything a reader needs, without CaveCAD.
  4. Ask yourself what is not in that zip, and why.

The rule you are being taught

A cave entrance's location does not travel by accident. The sanitized package drops the geographic anchor and the aerial photograph; your working drawing keeps both. So the file you hand over is the package, never the drawing you have been editing.

Truitt Cave reached you sanitized. That is why you have its shape, its trips and its sketches, and not its position.

Then put it back

Teaching Cave > Reset. The next student gets the cave as you found it.
