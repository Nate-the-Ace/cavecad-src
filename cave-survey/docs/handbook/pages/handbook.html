The documentation, inside the application. hb


One pane at a time: breadcrumb and back above, Contents and search below.


Contents, follow a link, Back, then search.

When you reach for it

When you do not know what a button does, what a word means, or what the next step is. Nothing here needs a network or a second window.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Handbook, or the ? on any panel, or type hb.
  2. Contents at the foot lists every page; the back arrow returns to the page you were on.
  3. Type in the search box and press Return. Pages whose title matches come first. One hit goes straight there.
  4. Every page ends with a Next: link, so the handbook can also be read front to back as a course.

When it refuses

  • The handbook is not installed — the pages ship beside the add-on and this build has none, usually a half-copied install. The panel names the folders it looked in.
