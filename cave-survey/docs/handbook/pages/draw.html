One panel for putting marks on the map: Trace, Symbols, Areas and Custom Linetypes. draw


Trace and Symbols sharing row one, Areas and Custom Linetypes row two. Anything that draws a line is a full-width button showing a strip of that line.


Fold a section and the rest of the dock reflows to fill the space.

When you reach for it

Whenever you are drawing. Cave drawing is one job done with one hand — a wall, then the ledge below it, then the gravel filling the floor between them — so the three used to be separate docks and you were choosing which panel to look at before choosing what to draw.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Draw, or ft, sym or area — all three open this panel with that section unfolded.
  2. Sections sit two to a row: Trace and Symbols share row one, Areas and Custom Linetypes row two.
  3. Custom Linetypes shows your own linetypes from Linetype Maker, grouped by the category you gave each one. Click one to draw with it; Escape puts your previous linetype back.
  4. Fold a section by its header; it keeps its cell rather than reflowing the grid.
  5. Right-click a header for Move Up, Move Down, Reset Order. What you fold and how you order is remembered.
