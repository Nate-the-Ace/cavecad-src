Add North Arrow

Puts a north arrow on the layout you are looking at. Click where it goes. It points north for the viewport under it (or the layout's only viewport), draws the magnetic arm when the survey has a declination, and turns when you rotate that viewport. northarrow

When you reach for it

When you are composing a sheet by hand and want a north arrow, or after you rotated a viewport and the sheet's own arrow is not the one you want.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only: in the cave itself it tells you to click a layout tab first.

It is the person's, not the generator's: Sheet Setup never rewrites or deletes a piece placed here.
