Work Check Map's list down to nothing

Fourteen checks over the drawing, each with a place and a reason. The list is meant to reach zero.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Check Map, then Check Again.
  2. Click the first finding. The drawing pans to it at the magnification you are working at, so walking the list walks the cave.
  3. Read why it matters. That half is the point: a beginner told "no scale bar" adds one; a beginner told what a scale bar is for checks for it on the next map unasked.
  4. Show Me reframes close up if the fault is small.
  5. Fix it, press Check Again, watch it go.
  6. Right-click a finding you have decided about and choose Ignore This.

What Ignore remembers

The finding's code and where it is, rounded to the foot. So deciding the gap at the entrance is deliberate says nothing about the other seven, and a fault that has genuinely moved comes back and asks again.

When it refuses

  • It changes nothing, ever. A finding may be a decision rather than a mistake.
  • A finding with nowhere to pan to — a missing scale bar is nowhere in particular — leaves the view alone.
