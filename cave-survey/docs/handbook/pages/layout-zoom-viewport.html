Zoom Viewport To Cave

Re-frames the selected viewport on what it is for: the whole cave, the elevation or the legend, at the first standard scale at which that fits its box. A locked viewport is left alone. zoomviewport

When you reach for it

After the cave grew, or after you resized or reshaped a viewport.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
