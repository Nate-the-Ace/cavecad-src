Symbol conventions

The names were never the hard part. The conventions are, and they are what gets a map marked down.

Where the conventions are written

On the symbol itself. Hover any tile in the Symbol Palette or the Trace section: the tooltip says what the thing is in one sentence, and then — emphasised — the rule that is easy to get backwards. The legend prints the same sentence, so a convention taught once is stated again on the sheet a reader holds.

The ones people reverse

  • Hachures point down the drop — on a ledge, a pit, a slope. Backwards, the map says the water runs uphill.
  • Rimstone scallops bow downslope, the way the water goes over the dam.
  • A pit is sized to its opening. Depth is a number in a callout, not a bigger symbol.
  • A dome is a shaft going up with no matching hole in the floor. A pit is the hole.

Your own symbols

They carry no explanation and never will: a symbol you drew means whatever you drew it to mean. The legend lists them without a sentence.
