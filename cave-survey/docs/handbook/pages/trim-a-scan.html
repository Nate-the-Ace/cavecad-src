Trim a scan to the sketch, not the page

A scanned field book page is mostly paper. Trimming leaves the drawn passage and drops the margin, the ruler, the facing page and the shadow of the binding.

Steps

  1. Select the scan in Sketch Scans.
  2. Press Trace and draw round the sketch — freehand, following the drawn passage rather than boxing it. Letting go closes the outline.
  3. Middle-drag still pans while you trace, so a long page does not have to be traced at one zoom.
  4. Undo and redo are on the same row if a stroke wanders.

The trim writes a derivative image beside the original; the original page is never altered. The derivative stays rectangular with transparency outside your outline, so everything downstream — the fit, the drape, a later relink — is unchanged.

Why bother

Two pages overlapping at their margins hide each other's passage. Trimmed, they lie side by side and the cave reads continuously.
