The privacy rule

A cave entrance's location is the one fact that must not travel by accident. Published entrances get vandalism, trash, injured novices and, often, a gate.

What the suite does about it

  • No coordinates are ever drawn on a map by these tools.
  • Package Cave Project is sanitized by default: the copied drawing loses its geographic anchor, and no aerial photograph travels.
  • Teaching Cave refuses to write a master at all unless it can strip both.
  • A plotted sheet carries no raster, which takes the aerial photograph with it.

What it cannot do about it

Your working drawing is not sanitized — it holds the anchor, and any imagery you fetched. Handing that file over hands over the entrance. So does a title block with a road name in it, and so does a photograph with camera GPS.

When in doubt, package it. That is what packaging is for.
