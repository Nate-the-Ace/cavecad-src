Add Sheet Index

Puts a sheet index on the layout: one row per layout with its paper and its scale. It is one block, anchored at its bottom left corner: click where that corner goes, and the index grows upward as sheets are added (the corner never moves). It follows the sheets by itself when one is added, renamed or removed. Adding it again replaces the earlier one on that layout. sheetindex

When you reach for it

Once the set of sheets is settled.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
