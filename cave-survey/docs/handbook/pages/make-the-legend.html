Make the legend say what the map uses

Generated from the drawing, not typed: the lines and symbols actually on this map, each with a sentence saying what it is.

Steps

  1. Finish drawing. A legend built early explains a map that has since changed.
  2. Cave Survey > Build Legend.
  3. Re-run it whenever the map gains a feature. Re-running replaces the legend in one undo step.

Everything lands on the LEGEND layer wearing its source layer's appearance, so it hides, moves and clears as one thing.

What a reader gets

Lines first — a reader meets the passage outline before the stalactites. Samples are drawn: a dashed inferred wall is shown dashed, a floor ledge with its hachures. The solid/dashed distinction is where a beginner reader goes wrong, because it looks like a drawing choice and is actually a statement about how much of the map was measured.

When it refuses

  • A feature the map does not use never appears — explaining a rimstone dam that is nowhere on the sheet sends a reader looking for it.
  • Your own symbols are listed but not explained.
  • For a tight sheet, switch the sentences off with CaveSurvey/LegendExplain.
