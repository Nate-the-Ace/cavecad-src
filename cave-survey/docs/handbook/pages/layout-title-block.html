Add Title Block

Puts the cave's title block on the layout. Click its lower left corner. It is filled in from the survey and from anything the drawing already says; the lines are ordinary text you can edit. titleblock

One block, with fields

The title block is one block: select it, move it, copy it to another sheet. Each line is a field (a block attribute): double-click a field to edit its text. The first field, Sheet, is the sheet's name, the same as its Layout tab: change either and the other follows.

Fields that follow the notebook

Cave name, surveyed by, dates, length, depth and survey code follow the survey notebook: when the notebook changes, Refresh from notebook (on the Sheet Item tab) brings them up to date. Type over one and it is yours: it is never overwritten again until you press Link field to notebook. Location is never filled in for you.

When you reach for it

On a hand-made sheet, or a second sheet that needs its own block.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only: in the cave itself it tells you to click a layout tab first.

It is the person's, not the generator's: Sheet Setup never rewrites or deletes a piece placed here.
