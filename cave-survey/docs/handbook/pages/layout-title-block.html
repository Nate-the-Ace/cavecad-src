Add Title Block

Puts the cave's title block on the layout. Click its lower left corner. It is filled in from the survey and from anything the drawing already says; the lines are ordinary text you can edit. titleblock

When you reach for it

On a hand-made sheet, or a second sheet that needs its own block.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only: in the cave itself it tells you to click a layout tab first.

It is the person's, not the generator's: Sheet Setup never rewrites or deletes a piece placed here.
