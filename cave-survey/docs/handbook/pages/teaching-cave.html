Teaching Cave

Hand a student a real cave they can safely ruin, and put it back afterwards. teach


Reset the student's copy, or set up the master from a real cave.

When you reach for it

Before a class, once, to make the master. Then after each student — or after your own mistake — to get a clean copy back.

Steps

  1. Set up takes a real cave once, removes its location with the same sanitizer Package Cave uses, and keeps the result as a pristine master under ~/Documents/Cave/teaching/master/.
  2. Reset throws the student's copy away and lays a fresh one down from that master. It asks first, because a reset takes somebody's evening away.

What travels, and what does not

Three folders, and the distinction is the whole design: the original wherever the surveyor keeps it, read once and never written to; the master, which resets copy from and never to; the working copy a student opens. A teaching cave that resets to something a student has been editing is not a teaching cave.

Field sketches travel — tracing a real sketch is most of what a student is here to learn. Plotted PDFs and photographs do not: a title block carries a location somebody typed, and a photograph carries wherever the camera thought it was.

When it refuses

  • Set up writes nothing at all if it cannot strip both the geo anchor and the aerial imagery. A half-sanitized cave is worse than none.
