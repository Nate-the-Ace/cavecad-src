Add Border

Puts a border round the layout you are looking at, 0.2 inch inside the paper's edge, on the border layer. A layout that already has one is told so. addborder

When you reach for it

On a hand-made sheet; the built-in templates and Sheet Setup draw their own.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
