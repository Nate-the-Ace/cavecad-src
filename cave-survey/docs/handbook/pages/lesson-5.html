Lesson 5 — Reading the map for faults

Everything you have drawn, read back by something that does not know what you meant.

Do this

  1. Cave Survey > Check Map, then Check Again.
  2. Click the first finding. The drawing pans to it. Read why it matters before you fix it.
  3. Fix it. Check Again. Watch it leave.
  4. Work down the list. Right-click anything you have decided is deliberate and Ignore This.
  5. Loop Errors. Read the arrows and the exaggeration in the caption, then switch CTRL-CLOSURE off.
  6. Build Legend.
  7. Sheet Setup: paper, scale, furniture, and read the warnings. Build Sheet.

What you should see

An empty findings list, a legend naming only what your map actually uses, and a sheet whose preview matched what came out.

If it goes wrong

  • A finding you cannot fix: it may be a decision rather than a mistake. Ignore it deliberately, not by switching the tool off.
  • Something off the paper: Sheet Setup says which thing. Change the scale or the paper.
  • Your legend explains something you cannot find on the map: you drew it somewhere and forgot. That is the legend doing its job.

Words used here

  • Loop closure and adjustment
  • Layers: what the map is saying
