Lesson 1 — Your first cave map

Get a real cave on screen and learn to look at it. Nothing is drawn in this lesson.

Before you start

Cave Survey > Teaching Cave > Set up, once. That gives you a copy of Truitt Cave you can ruin, with its location removed. Reset puts it back at any point in any lesson.

Do this

  1. Open Cave Survey > Caves. Find Truitt in the rail.
  2. Read the trip table. Each row is a day underground: date, team, how many shots, and the stations they stopped at. The last column is where the cave is still going.
  3. Read the health line: depth, stations, loops, UIS grade.
  4. Open the drawing.
  5. Cave Survey > Survey Stats. Note the length, the depth and the closure.
  6. Cave Survey > 3D View. Orbit the cave. Colour it by trip, and watch the survey grow with the play button.

What you should see

A centreline with a passage lofted around it, in several colours — one per trip. The map you are about to draw is a picture of this.

Questions to answer for yourself

  • Which trip found the biggest passage?
  • Where does the cave still have open ends?
  • Does it close? By how much, and does that number mean anything on a cave this size?

Words used here

  • Station, shot, splay, LRUD
  • Loop closure and adjustment
