Privacy

Nothing to accept. This page lists every time CaveCAD goes online and what happens to anything you send.

What stays on your computer

Drawings, survey data, scans and session logs never leave your computer unless you send them with Help > Send Feedback.

When CaveCAD goes online

  • Check for Updates asks GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address, nothing else.
  • Surface Data and the aerial basemap ask the USGS National Map. It sees your IP address and the area fetched, which is roughly where your cave is.
  • Entrance Location shows OpenStreetMap and Esri imagery, and loads its map library from unpkg.com. They see your IP address and the area you view.
  • Send Feedback sends only what you tick, to Google Drive under cavecad.app@gmail.com.

The map services never receive cave names or survey data.

Send Feedback

  • A report holds what you write, CaveCAD's version, your operating system, the tool in use and the names of your open drawings, plus whatever you tick. Session logs record the commands you used and the file paths of drawings you opened, which can include your computer's user name.
  • Only the CaveCAD maintainer and people they trust read reports. Never published, sold or passed on.
  • Kept until 30 days after the report is closed, and never more than 12 months.
  • To have a report deleted, email cavecad.app@gmail.com with its reference.
  • Your drawing stays yours; sending it lets us use it to fix the problem, nothing else.
