When you reach for it

When someone asks how long the cave is, and before you put a grade in a title block.

Reading the closure number

"Closes at 2.4%" is not a grade you passed or failed. It says walking the loop and coming back landed you 2.4% of the way you walked from where you started. Which stations moved, how far, and which way is the whole story — and it is invisible on the map until you draw it.
