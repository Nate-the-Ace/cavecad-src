Callout

A text note bound to one or more leader arrows. cal


Place the note, then an arrow to each thing it is about.

When you reach for it

For anything the map has to say in words: a depth, a name, a lead, a warning.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Callout. It opens on a choice of where the text comes from.
  2. Type it, or pick a point and let it read a spot floor elevation off the LRUD and splays there — not off the survey line.
  3. Place the note, then the arrows.

QCAD has no multileader, so this is a real text entity and a real leader per arrow, linked: the note stays text-editable and the arrows can be reflowed after a move.

When it refuses

  • A floor elevation with no measurement to read stamps LINE on its face, saying it fell back to the survey line.
  • Arrows stranded by a moved or reworded note are put back by Repair Drawing.
