Set a student up on the teaching cave, and reset it after

A real cave to learn on, with the location removed, and a way to put it back when they have wrecked it.

Once, before the class

  1. Cave Survey > Teaching Cave > Set up, and point it at the real cave.
  2. It strips the location and keeps a pristine master under ~/Documents/Cave/teaching/master/.
  3. The working copy is registered on the shelf, so it opens like any other cave.

Between students

  1. Teaching Cave > Reset. It asks first, because a reset takes somebody's evening away.
  2. The student's copy is thrown away and a fresh one laid down from the master.

What travels

Field sketches do — tracing a real sketch is most of what a student is here to learn, and an invented cave can never have one. Plotted PDFs and photographs do not: a title block carries a location somebody typed, and a photograph carries wherever the camera thought it was.

When it refuses

  • Set up writes nothing at all if it cannot strip both the geo anchor and the aerial imagery.
  • Reset copies from the master and never to it. A teaching cave that reset to something a student had been editing would not be a teaching cave.

Wrecked one drawing, not the cave

Reset Drawing empties a drawing while keeping the folder, the scans and the shelf entry — the smaller hammer, and usually the right one.
