Lesson 4 — Sections and the profile

Two more views of the same cave, and both are drawn the same way you drew the plan.

Do this

  1. Cave Survey > Generate Profile. The extended elevation appears beside the plan, one band per survey run.
  2. Trace a stretch of floor and ceiling inside a band's box. Notice you did not pick a view — where you drew decided.
  3. Cross Section, default route: click a point on the passage, then where the section goes. Read the caption's distance from the nearer station.
  4. Cross Section again, Trace a scanned section. Scale the scan onto the dashed ghost, trace it, press Capture, and place it.
  5. 3D View. Your sections are standing beside the passage they were cut from.

What you should see

A profile that reads like the cave seen from the side, and sections that face the right way because the cut carried its reference from leg to leg.

If it goes wrong

  • The bay opened with no ghost: that station has no cuttable LRUD. Trace anyway, or pick another station.
  • Capture refused: nothing is traced inside the frame yet.
  • The profile sits a thousand feet below the plan on a sheet: untick it in Sheet Setup, or build the profile sheet.

Words used here
