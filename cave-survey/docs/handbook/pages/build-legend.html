Build Legend

Generate the legend from what the map actually uses — its lines as well as its symbols — and say what each one means. bl

When you reach for it

Last, before the sheet. Re-run it whenever the map gains a feature it did not have.

What it makes

Three kinds of row: traced feature layers (surveyed and inferred walls, ceiling, floor, breakdown), shaped lines drawn with their ornament, and catalogue symbols with NSS names and UIS aliases. Lines come first, because a reader meets the passage outline before the stalactites.

The samples are drawn, not described. "Inferred Walls" as words teaches nobody which of the lines on the map is the inferred one — and the solid/dashed distinction is exactly where a beginner goes wrong, because it looks like a drawing choice and is in fact a statement about how much of the map was measured.

Under each name sits one plain sentence saying what the thing is — the same words the Feature Trace and Symbol Palette tooltips carry. Switch the sentences off with CaveSurvey/LegendExplain for a tight sheet.

When it refuses

  • Nothing is invented: a feature the map does not use does not appear. A legend explaining a rimstone dam that is nowhere on the sheet sends a reader looking for it.
  • Your own symbols are listed but never explained — they mean whatever you drew them to mean.
