Save Layout As Template

Saves the layout you are looking at as a template: where its viewports are, their shapes, scales, rotation and hidden layers, and where the north arrow, scale bar and title block sit. Keep it with this cave (a Layout templates folder beside the drawing, so the team gets it through the cave folder) or just for you. savelayout

When you reach for it

When you have composed a sheet you like and want the next one to look the same.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu.

Templates are plain files, one each, named for the template. A cave's own template of the same name wins over a built-in one.
