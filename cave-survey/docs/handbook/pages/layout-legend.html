Add Legend

Puts the map's legend on the layout. Click where its top left goes. The legend is not copied: it is a viewport onto the legend Build Legend drew in the model, hiding every other layer, at the first standard scale at which it fits in about a third of the sheet. The sheet's other viewports hide the legend layer, so it never lands on the map. Build the legend first (in the model: Cave Survey > Build Legend); rebuilding it updates the sheet. addlegend

When you reach for it

When the sheet needs its key.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu. It works on a layout only.
