Area Fill

Fill the cave floor with a pattern — gravel, sand, breakdown, water, flowstone and eight others. area


Search the patterns, then set Scale and Density.

When you reach for it

For what the floor is made of. It is the Areas section of the Draw panel, not a panel of its own.

Steps

  1. Pick a pattern from the tile grid. Each tile is a picture of what that pattern actually draws, not a stand-in: a scatter pattern shows the real generator's placements, a filled pattern shows its wash or ruled hatch, and bedrock — which draws no fill at all — shows an empty frame.
  2. Press and drag to enclose the boundary. Release and it fills, in one undo step with the boundary.
  3. Where you drag decides the view, the same rule Feature Trace and the Symbol Palette use.
  4. Scale and Density size and thin what gets placed.

Edit the boundary afterwards with QCAD's own tools and the fill regenerates to match; delete the boundary and its fill goes with it. The seed is kept for the life of the boundary, so a scatter's look survives an edit, an undo, a reload or a redraw on someone else's machine.

When it refuses

  • Density is disabled while a filled pattern is armed: a hatch has nothing scattered in it to thin out.
