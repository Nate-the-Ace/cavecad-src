Lesson 3 — Drawing walls over a scan

The centreline is the skeleton. This is the cave.

Do this

  1. Sketch Scans, Plan tab. Pick a page and press Align on Scan.
  2. Click two stations on the scan and name them. The page lands fitted under the survey.
  3. Open the Draw panel.
  4. Arm Surveyed Walls and trace one wall of a passage, in as many passes as it takes. Start each pass where the last one stopped.
  5. Trace the other wall.
  6. Arm Inferred Walls and trace a stretch the surveyor did not measure. Notice it comes out dashed.
  7. Arm Floor Ledge, drag along a lip, and click the low side.
  8. In the Areas section, fill the floor with gravel.
  9. In Symbols, place an entrance symbol, and a flow arrow by dragging rather than clicking.

What you should see

Each wall one line, not six. The ledge's hachures hanging down the drop. The gravel following the boundary when you drag a grip.

If it goes wrong

  • Six lines instead of one: your next pass did not start where the last stopped. Ctrl+Z and try again, or use Shift at the press.
  • Hachures the wrong way: shf flips them.
  • The line landed in the wrong view: look at the readout above the tiles before you press — where you drag decides.
