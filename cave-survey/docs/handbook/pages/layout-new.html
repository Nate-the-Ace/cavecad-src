New Layout From Template

Makes a new layout from a template and shows it. Pick the template (built in, this cave's own, or yours), name the layout, and it is built: the paper, its viewports at a scale that fits the cave, the border, the north arrow, scale bar and title block. The + beside the layout tabs does the same. newlayout

When you reach for it

Every sheet after the first, and every new cave: start from the house style instead of a blank page.

Where it is

The Layout menu and the Cave Survey menu.

Templates are plain files, one each, named for the template. A cave's own template of the same name wins over a built-in one.
