Get a scan to line up under the drawing

A scanned sketch has to sit where the survey says it sits, or every wall traced off it is wrong by the same amount.

The short way: Align on Scan

  1. Cave Survey > Sketch Scans, and pick the Plan tab.
  2. Select the page in the tree. Hover any scan for a preview.
  3. Press Align on Scan. The page opens in a zoomable viewer: wheel to zoom, middle-drag to pan.
  4. Left-click a station on the scan — the pencil mark the surveyor wrote, not a guess — and name it from the drawing's own list. Do two.
  5. Press through, and the scan is placed already fitted. No insert-then-hunt-then-align.

The other way: fit it by hand

Use it when the scan’s station marks cannot be read on the page, or when the scan is easier to read at drawing size. Add a Scan and Fit by Hand... — or a double-click on the row in the list — drops the page over the survey at a guessed size, then: click a point on the image, click where it belongs in the drawing, repeat. It moves, rotates and resizes to match.

How many stations

  • Two — a plain fit: position, rotation, one scale.
  • Three or more — also takes out the scanner's own stretch and skew, and reports how far the worst station missed by.

Read that miss. A big one usually means a station was named wrong, not that the sketch is bad.

A page that is not in the list

The same Add a Scan and Fit by Hand... button file-picks it — a photo still on a phone, a page scanned to the Desktop — into the same fit. Or drag image files straight onto the preview: they are copied into the selected trip's folder, never moved, and never over a file already there.

Try it on Truitt

Line up one plan page with two stations, then do the same page again with four. Compare the reported miss.
