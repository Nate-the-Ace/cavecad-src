Lesson 2 — Typing a trip into the notebook

Shots on paper become a cave in a drawing.

Do this

  1. Open Survey Notebook.
  2. Press Scan and pick a page of Truitt's field book. It sits under the shot ladder, on a splitter you can drag.
  3. Type a short trip off that page: station names, distance, azimuth, inclination, then LRUD.
  4. Set the date and the team.
  5. Open Declination and estimate it from the survey date.
  6. Draw.

What you should see

New stations and legs, tied into the station you started from, and the stats and closure updating as you typed rather than after.

If it goes wrong

  • A shot refused: the station it starts from is not in the drawing. Check the name.
  • The whole cave redrew: something moved existing stations. A corrected declination does that, and it is correct.
  • Two copies of your trip: you retyped it instead of loading it from the drawing. See Correct a bad reading.

Then undo it

This was practice on somebody's real survey. Ctrl+Z back to where you started, or Teaching Cave > Reset.
